Privacy Policy

Oona Panthu  ·  Effective date: May 30, 2025

Oona Panthu ("we", "our", or "the app") is a sports scoring and tournament management application for the traditional game of Oona Panthu. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Who This App Is For

Oona Panthu is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately so we can delete their data.

2. Information We Collect

Account & Profile

  • Email address and password (if you register with email) — used for authentication only.
  • Google account name and profile photo (if you sign in with Google) — provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Display name and optional location you enter in your profile.

App Content You Create

  • Teams and player rosters you create.
  • Match records, scores, and ball-by-ball events.
  • Tournament brackets and results.

Device & Usage

  • Profile photos you choose from your device's photo library (only when you explicitly select an image; we do not scan your gallery).
  • Basic crash and performance data collected automatically by Firebase (e.g. device model, OS version, app version). This contains no personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Display your name and profile in matches and tournaments you participate in.
  • Show real-time match scores and live spectator counts to other signed-in users.
  • Aggregate career statistics (matches played, points scored) on your profile.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

We do not use your data for advertising or share it with ad networks.

4. How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore database, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Storage), hosted in the United States. Firebase is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google's security and compliance standards. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Match and tournament data you create is visible to any signed-in user of the app — scores and fixtures are public within the app. Your email address is never shown to other users.

5. Data Sharing

We share data only in these limited cases:

  • Google Firebase — our backend infrastructure (authentication, database, storage).
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google; governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirement — if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

6. Permissions We Request

  • Photos / Media Storage — only to let you select a profile photo. We do not read any other files on your device.
  • Internet — required to sync matches, tournaments, and user data with Firebase.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your profile at any time within the app.
  • Delete your account — contact us at the email below and we will delete your account and all associated personal data within 30 days.
  • Opt out of Google Sign-In — use email/password registration instead.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Match and tournament records may be retained for historical/statistical purposes even after account deletion, but will be de-identified (your name replaced with "Deleted User").

9. Security

We use Firebase's built-in security rules to ensure users can only read and write data they are authorised to access. Passwords are hashed by Firebase Authentication and are never stored or visible to us in plain text.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the "Effective date" at the top will be updated. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
